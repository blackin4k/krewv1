# Krew Music Sourcing & Content Policy

## Overview
This platform is intended for legitimate distribution and playback of content where you (the uploader) have the proper rights, licenses, or authorizations.

## Policy Rules
1. **Forbidden Operations**: The direct extraction, scraping, or automated downloading of commercial or copyrighted tracks from third-party services (e.g., YouTube, Spotify) via tools like `yt-dlp` or similar mechanisms is strictly prohibited.
2. **Uploading**: By uploading any audio files or artwork, you attest that you are the creator of the work, or hold the necessary rights to distribute and perform the work publicly on this platform.
3. **Takedowns**: We strictly adhere to the Digital Millennium Copyright Act (DMCA). Any material found to be infringing on copyrights without proper authorization will be immediately and irrevocably removed from the platform and storage buckets.
4. **Repeat Infringers**: Accounts that are repeatedly flagged or involved in unauthorized distribution of copyrighted material will be permanently terminated.

## DMCA Notifications
Contact the administrators immediately through our support channels for any copyright takedown requests. Please include the link to the infringing material and your proof of ownership.
